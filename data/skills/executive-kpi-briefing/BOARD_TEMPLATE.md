# Executive Briefing — Board Template

## Output Format

EXECUTIVE BRIEFING — [Date]

### HEADLINE KPIs
- Total Firm AUM: [FIRM_AUM] (from holdings, authoritative)
- Net Flows: [amount] (Inflows: [x], Outflows: [y])
- Active Clients: [count]
- As of: [latest data date]

### STRATEGY PERFORMANCE

| Strategy | AUM | QTD Return | YTD Return | Flows | Board View |
|----------|-----|------------|------------|-------|------------|
| [Sorted by performance] | | | | |

**TOP PERFORMERS**: [list top 3 with brief rationale and whether flows confirm momentum]

**AREAS OF CONCERN**: [list any underperforming strategies, negative-flow strategies, or concentration risks]

### CLIENT INSIGHTS
- Flow concentration: [broad-based or concentrated in few clients]
- Key segments driving flows: [pension, endowment, etc.]
- Any single client >10% of total flows: [flag if yes]
- Commercial quality of flows: [sticky/diversified or fragile/concentrated]

### STRATEGIC CONTEXT
- [Key philosophy alignment points from internal docs]
- [Strategic positioning summary]

### KEY MESSAGES FOR BOARD
1. [Main takeaway — lead with the headline number]
2. [Performance vs flow interpretation — where momentum is strongest or weakest]
3. [Primary risk or concentration watch item, including whether follow-up client drill-down is needed]
4. [Any required actions or decisions needed]

### RECOMMENDED NEXT QUESTION
- [If flows look strong in a strategy, suggest checking whether demand is broad-based or concentrated]
- [If a strategy is weak but strategically important, suggest drilling into drivers and client behaviour]
