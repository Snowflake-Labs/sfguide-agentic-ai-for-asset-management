# RFP Response Template Structure

## 1. Executive Summary
Key highlights, value proposition, and mandate fit in 3-5 bullet points.

## 2. Firm Overview
AUM, history, regulatory status, office locations, ownership structure.

## 3. Investment Process and Philosophy
Multi-stage process description, decision-making framework, team governance.

## 4. Performance Track Record
Returns vs benchmark (QTD, YTD, longer-term where available), risk metrics.

## 5. Portfolio Characteristics
Top holdings, sector allocation, style characteristics, turnover.

## 6. Risk Management
Risk limits, monitoring framework, escalation procedures.

## 7. Compliance and Regulatory
Registrations, policies, relevant regulatory disclosures (SFDR, TCFD, etc.).

## 8. Team and Resources
Key personnel with [TO COMPLETE] markers for named biographies.

## 9. Operational Infrastructure
Custody, administration, audit, technology platform.

## 10. Fee Proposal
[TO COMPLETE] — Standard fee schedule format with placeholders.

## 11. Appendices
Supporting data tables, detailed performance statistics, disclaimers.
